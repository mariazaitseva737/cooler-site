## What happens

Hormone swings affect mood directly. Add poor sleep, hot flashes, and the fact that these years often bring a lot at once: work, aging parents, grown children.

## Brain fog

You struggle to find a word, to concentrate, things slip your mind. In studies, this improved for most women once the transition was over. It helps to get enough sleep, write important things down, do one thing at a time, and keep moving.

## When it is depression

If for two weeks or longer you feel low or nothing brings you joy nearly every day, and you also have trouble with sleep or appetite, no energy, or feelings of guilt or worthlessness, it may be depression. It is not a weakness of character, and it responds well to therapy, medication or both.

## What helps day to day

- Movement: even a 20-minute walk lifts your mood.
- Sleep: there is a separate [article on sleep](/en/blog/sleep-after-menopause/).
- People: talking to a friend helps more than you might think.
- CBT helps with both mood swings and anxiety.
