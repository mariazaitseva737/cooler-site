## What changes

Estrogen helped protect your blood vessels. After menopause, “bad” cholesterol (LDL) and blood pressure often go up, and fat starts to settle around the belly. The American Heart Association advises paying closer attention to heart health in exactly these years.

## What to check

- Blood pressure. A home monitor is handy: measure at least once a week.
- A lipid panel, which is the cholesterol test.
- Blood sugar or HbA1c.
- Waist size. It is an easy way to keep an eye on belly fat.

## What helps

About 150 minutes of moderate activity a week, for example a brisk 30-minute walk five days a week. Not smoking. Enough sleep. Less salt. If your blood pressure or cholesterol is high, treat it: that is not “pills because of age”, it is real protection for your heart.

## What a heart attack looks like in women

It is not always a crushing chest pain. It can be pressure or discomfort in the chest, pain in the jaw, back or arm, shortness of breath, nausea, a cold sweat, or sudden extreme tiredness.
